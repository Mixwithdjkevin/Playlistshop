FAQ

ACADEMY QUESTIONS

Do I need prior musical experience to join?

No prior musical background is required. We start from ground zero: understanding music bar structures, BPM counting, and equipment fundamentals.

What is the difference between Online and Offline?

Online courses are live 1-on-1 video sessions with access to the student learning portal, making them suitable for students anywhere in the world.

Offline courses take place physically at DJ Kevin's Mumbai studio, where students get hands-on training using Pioneer XDJ-RR and DDJ-400 equipment along with practical Rekordbox and DJ performance training.

Do I get a completion certificate?

Yes! All graduates receive an official Musik N Beatz Certified Pro DJ Certificate along with a recorded 60-minute promo mix.
