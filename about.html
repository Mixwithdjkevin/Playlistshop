ABOUT DJ KEVIN

DJ Kevin is a professional DJ, educator, and content creator with years of experience performing at weddings, private events, clubs, and corporate functions. Through Mix With DJ Kevin, he helps DJs perform with confidence by providing professionally organized Rekordbox and Serato playlists, hot cue collections, DJ edits, and practical training.

After spending years organizing and preparing music for real-world performances, Kevin recognized how difficult it was to find clean, properly tagged, and performance-ready DJ libraries. That's why he created Mix With DJ Kevin—a platform dedicated to delivering high-quality playlists, gain-balanced audio, pre-mapped hot cues, and hands-on DJ education through Musik N Beatz Academy, along with tutorials and resources for DJs of all skill levels.
